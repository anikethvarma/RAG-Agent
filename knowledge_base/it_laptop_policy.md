# IT Equipment & Laptop Policy

Owner: IT Team

## Standard laptops
Engineers receive a MacBook Pro 16-inch with 48 GB of RAM. All other roles receive a MacBook Pro 14-inch with 24 GB of RAM.

## Refresh cycle
Laptops are refreshed every 36 months. Early replacement is only granted for hardware failure.

## Lost or stolen devices
A lost or stolen laptop or phone must be reported within 1 hour of noticing the loss, by posting in the #it-help Slack channel and by emailing security@helixcloud.example. IT will remotely lock and wipe the device.
