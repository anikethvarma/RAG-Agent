# Office Locations

Helix Cloud has three hub offices: Austin (United States), Dublin (Ireland), and Singapore.

## Office hours
Hub offices are open from 07:00 to 20:00 local time on working days. The Dublin office is closed on Irish public holidays.

## Visitors
Visitors must be registered at least 24 hours in advance through the front-desk system and must be accompanied by an employee at all times.
