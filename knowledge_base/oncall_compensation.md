# On-call Compensation

Owner: Engineering Operations

## Weekly stipend
Engineers on the primary on-call rotation receive a stipend of $350 per week. Engineers on the secondary rotation receive $150 per week.

## Time in lieu
If you are paged outside working hours and the incident takes more than 1 hour of work, you receive time in lieu equal to the time worked (1:1), to be taken within the following 30 days.

## Rotation length
On-call rotations last one week, starting Monday at 10:00 local time.
