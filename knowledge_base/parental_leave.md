# Parental Leave Policy

Owner: People Team

## Leave entitlement
The primary caregiver receives 18 weeks of fully paid parental leave. The secondary caregiver receives 8 weeks of fully paid parental leave. Leave may be taken within 12 months of the birth, adoption, or foster placement.

## Eligibility
Employees become eligible for paid parental leave after completing 6 months of continuous employment at Helix Cloud. Employees with less than 6 months of tenure may take unpaid leave as required by local law.

## Returning to work
Returning parents may work a reduced 60% schedule at full pay for their first 4 weeks back.
