# Paid Time Off (PTO) Policy

Owner: People Team · Applies to: all full-time Helix Cloud employees

## Annual allowance
Full-time employees receive 22 days of paid time off per calendar year. PTO accrues monthly at a rate of 1.83 days per month, starting on the employee's first day.

## Requesting PTO
Requests are submitted in the Orbit HR portal. Absences longer than 3 consecutive working days must be requested at least 10 business days in advance and approved by your manager.

## Carryover
Up to 5 unused PTO days may be carried over into the next calendar year. Carried-over days must be used by March 31; any carried-over days not used by then are forfeited. Unused PTO is not paid out except where local law requires it.
