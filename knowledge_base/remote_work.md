# Remote & Hybrid Work Policy

Owner: Workplace Team

## Hybrid schedule
Employees assigned to a hub office work in the office two days per week, on Tuesday and Thursday. Fully remote arrangements require approval from a VP.

## Home office stipend
Every remote or hybrid employee receives a one-time home office stipend of $600 to buy a desk, chair, monitor, or other equipment. In addition, Helix Cloud reimburses home internet at $45 per month. Both are claimed in Ledgerly, the expense tool.

## Working from another country
Working from a country other than your country of employment is allowed for up to 30 days per calendar year and must be registered with the People Team in advance.
