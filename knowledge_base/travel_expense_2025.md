# Travel & Expense Policy 2025

Status: CURRENT. Effective January 1, 2025. Replaces the 2023 Travel Policy.

## Hotels
The standard hotel cap is $260 per night. In high-cost cities — New York, London, and San Francisco — the cap is $320 per night.

## Meals
The daily meal per diem for business travel is $75.

## Flights
Economy class is the default. Business class is allowed for flights with a scheduled duration longer than 8 hours.

## Submitting expenses
All expenses must be submitted in Ledgerly with itemised receipts within 30 days of the expense date. Late submissions require Finance approval.
